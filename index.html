Arafat PM

A private, offline-first password manager for Android

Arafat PM stores your usernames and passwords in a vault that is encrypted on your phone with AES-256-GCM. The key is protected by your master password (Argon2id) and, optionally, a 24-word recovery phrase. There are no ads, no analytics and no accounts. The developer cannot see your data.

Features

  • Master password, fingerprint unlock, automatic lock
  • Strong password generator and autofill in other apps and websites
  • Optional weekly encrypted backup to a hidden folder of your own Google Drive
