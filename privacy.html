Who we are

Arafat Password Manager is a personal password manager developed by Yasin Arafat. Contact: 29.arafat@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The app stores the entries you add (site or app names, usernames, passwords, trading-account details, notes) in a single vault file inside the app’s private storage on your phone. The file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is written. Your master password and recovery phrase are never stored in readable form and are never sent anywhere. We do not receive, collect or have access to any of your vault data.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no crash-reporting services.
  • No user accounts and no servers operated by the developer.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn it on, the app uploads a copy of the already-encrypted vault file to the hidden “App data” folder of your own Google Drive, using the permission drive.appdata. This permission lets the app see only its own hidden folder, never your other files. The file is unreadable without your master password or recovery phrase. The developer cannot access your Google Drive or your backups. You can disconnect at any time in the app and delete the backups in your Google Account settings (Data & privacy, third-party apps, delete hidden app data).

The app’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Autofill

When you enable Arafat Password Manager as your autofill service, Android shows the app the structure of the login screen you are using (field types and the website or app name) so it can fill in or save a login. This information is used only on your phone, at that moment, and is not stored or transmitted.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only for the optional Google Drive backup and, in the downloadable (non-Play) build, to check the public GitHub releases page for app updates. No personal data or vault data is sent in update checks.

Clipboard

If you tap a copy button, the value is placed on the clipboard marked as sensitive and removed after 30 seconds.

Your choices and deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes the vault from your phone. Backups in Google Drive stay until you delete them in your Google Account settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will be published on this page.
